Verified by comparing the FY 2027 guidelines (effective Oct 1, 2026) with the FY 2026 guidelines (effective Oct 1, 2025) published by CMS. Only these differences were found; do not claim any others.
1. Section I.C.9.a.1 (Hypertension with Heart Disease): both paragraphs now read "Hypertension with one or more heart conditions classified to ..." (FY 2026 said "Hypertension with heart conditions classified to ..."). The sentence about I13 vs I11/I12 and the acute renal failure sentence were split into two sentences (no change in meaning).
2. Section I.C.9.a.10 (Hypertensive Crisis): "Code also any identified hypertensive disease (I10-I15, I1A)" — I1A (resistant hypertension) was added to the range.
3. Chapter 17 title and guideline now include "genetic disorders": "Congenital malformations, deformations, chromosomal abnormalities, and genetic disorders (Q00-QA1)", and each sentence now says "malformation/deformation, chromosomal abnormality, or genetic disorder".
4. Section I.C.21.c (History Z codes list): NEW code Z84.A, Family history of exposure to diethylstilbestrol (DES), with a note: assigned when a family member was exposed to DES that could put the patient at higher risk (example: the patient's maternal grandmother was exposed to DES).
5. Section I.C.21.c (Miscellaneous Z codes list): a NEW note under Z91.B, Personal risk factor of exposure to diethylstilbestrol: assigned to show the patient was directly exposed to DES in utero (e.g., the patient's mother was exposed to DES while the patient was in utero).
6. Wording-only edits (no change in rule): Section I.C.1.a.2.a now names B20 as "Human immunodeficiency virus [HIV] disease"; Section I.C.1.g.1.g adds commas after R06.02 and R50.9 in its symptom list; Chapter 3 title "Disease" -> "Diseases"; Chapter 18 intro sentence; Z05 newborn observation sentence ("no condition is found to be present after study"); Appendix I (POA) sentences ("A POA indicator is assigned to the principal and secondary diagnoses", "query the provider", "the POA indicator is not applicable").
